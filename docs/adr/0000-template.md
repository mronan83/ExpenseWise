# ADR-NNNN: Title in a few words

One sentence that states the decision.

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-NNNN (link to the newer ADR)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** Who made or approved the decision
- **Decision register:** D-NN, if the decision comes from the blueprint's register

## Context

What problem forces a decision now. State the constraints, the forces in tension and the facts that matter. Link to the journey, capability or risk that raised it.

## Decision

What we will do, in active voice: "We will ...". Include the scope, the phase it applies to and anything that is explicitly out of scope.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Why not chosen |
| --- | --- |
| Alternative A | Reason |
| Alternative B | Reason |

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets easier or safer.

### Negative

- What gets harder, costs more or carries risk, and how we contain it.

## Exit path / reversibility

How we would back out or change course: what is behind an interface or adapter, what a migration would involve, and what would make us revisit this decision.

## Links

- Related docs, ADRs, risks and external references.
